Annual Harvest & Michaelmas Festival Celebrated at Roshni Village

Residents of Roshni Village, children of Green Earth School, along with visiting parents and international volunteers gathered to celebrate our annual Autumn Harvest. Fresh whole-grain bread baked in our wood-fired stone oven was shared amidst theater, songs, and lantern walks.
