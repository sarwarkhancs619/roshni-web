Three Core Projects

Our Core Initiatives

Three interconnected programs — a residential village with vocational workshops, a free Waldorf school, and a rural rehabilitation outreach.

🏡 Residential Community

Roshni Village

Pakistan's first intentional residential community for adults with intellectual disabilities — peaceful cottages, organic meals, 24/7 care, and 5 vocational workshops within the campus.

Explore Village & Workshops
🏫 Waldorf Education

Green Earth School

Pakistan's pioneering Waldorf school providing 100% free, holistic education (Kindergarten to Grade 8), nutritious meals, and art for rural village children.

Discover Green Earth
Reaching Rural Families

Community Based Rehabilitation

Reaching disabled individuals and their families in surrounding rural villages — providing free medical, educational, and psychosocial support right at their doorstep.
