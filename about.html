About Roshni Association

Dedicated to creating a loving, self-reliant community where people with intellectual disabilities and special needs live with dignity, self-respect, and creative expression.

Our Purpose

Nurturing the Whole Human Being

Roshni Association was founded in 1991 in Lahore, Pakistan, inspired by the worldwide Camphill Movement and anthroposophical curative education initiated by Rudolf Steiner.

In traditional society, individuals with intellectual challenges often face isolation or pity. At Roshni, we see beyond outer disabilities to celebrate the intact spiritual individuality of each human being.

Our Mission

To provide life-sharing community homes, meaningful vocational craft workshops, biodynamic farming, and free holistic Waldorf schooling for disadvantaged children.

Our Vision

A harmonious, compassionate society in Pakistan where people of all physical and intellectual abilities are embraced as valuable, contributing partners in life.
