Core Residential Community

Roshni Village

Roshni Village is Pakistan's first intentional residential community for adults with intellectual disabilities. Situated along Bedian Road in Lahore, it provides a tranquil, family-like setting where special persons live, work, and socialise in an atmosphere of mutual respect.

Residents live in community family houses alongside dedicated house parents and co-workers, sharing healthy organic meals and vibrant cultural celebrations. Within the village campus, residents participate every day in five meaningful vocational workshops which bring structure, accomplishment, and joy.

  • 24/7 loving residential care and medical oversight
  • Daily structured therapeutic rhythm and seasonal festivals
  • Nutritious organic meals harvested from our biodynamic farm
  • Art, music, eurythmy movement, and social companionship
  • 5 daily vocational workshops run by residents themselves

Roshni Organic Bakery

Our bakery is one of Roshni's most beloved therapeutic enterprises. Fresh whole-wheat sourdough loaves, rye breads, and wholesome treats are prepared daily by special residents alongside master bakers.

  • 100% organic, chemical-free whole grains
  • 24-hour slow natural sourdough fermentation
  • Stone-ground flour milled on the village premises
  • Available for order — delivered across Lahore

Biodynamic Farm & Dairy

Spanning verdant acres along Bedian Road, our biodynamic farm is cultivated completely free from synthetic pesticides. Residents participate in sowing, tending, and harvesting — building connection with the living earth.

  • Seasonal vegetables, wheat, and organic honey
  • Farm-to-table meals for residents and school children
  • Composting, crop rotation & natural soil regeneration
  • Gentle dairy herd providing fresh milk daily

Free Rural Waldorf Education

Green Earth Roshni School

Founded to bring Rudolf Steiner's Waldorf philosophy to underprivileged children of rural Lahore. We provide 100% free schooling, uniforms, books, and daily nutritious meals from Kindergarten to Grade 8.

Located directly within the Roshni Village campus, Green Earth School bridges mainstream village children and special adults — fostering empathy, tolerance, and inclusion from early childhood.

Rural Outreach • 300 Beneficiaries

Community Based Rehabilitation (CBR)

The CBR program reaches over 300 disabled individuals and their families in surrounding rural villages around Bedian Road, Lahore — providing free medical, educational, livelihood, and social inclusion support right at their doorstep.

Bringing Rehabilitation to the Doorstep

Many families with disabled members cannot access conventional rehabilitation services due to poverty, distance, or stigma. The CBR team visits them in their homes and communities, providing:

  • Physical therapy and mobility aid assessments
  • Psychological counselling and family guidance sessions
  • Medical referrals and health screenings for disabled persons
  • Educational support & school enrollment facilitation
  • Livelihood training for parents of disabled children
  • Awareness workshops to reduce disability stigma in villages

Five Pillars of our CBR Approach

🏥

Health Inclusion

Medical screenings, therapy referrals, and assistive devices for mobility and hearing.

📚

Education Inclusion

Enrollment support, special learning materials, and home tutoring for disabled children.

💼

Livelihood

Vocational training and micro-enterprise support for families of persons with disabilities.

🤝

Social Inclusion

Community awareness campaigns and anti-stigma workshops in local schools and mosques.

🏛️

Empowerment

Rights education, legal aid awareness, and connecting families to government support schemes.
