Frequently Asked Questions

Is Roshni Association a registered charity?

Yes, registered under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860 in Pakistan.

Can I purchase organic bread & crafts on site?

Yes, fresh organic bread and craft items can be purchased during visits or ordered via our online shop.

Are donations tax-exempt?

Yes, tax deduction status is available under section 2(36) of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001.
