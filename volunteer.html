🤝 Live, Learn & Serve Together

Volunteer With Roshni Association

Share your skills, open your heart, and experience life inside an inclusive village community on Bedian Road, Lahore.

Opportunities

Where You Can Make an Impact

We welcome students, professionals, artists, retirees, and international volunteers looking for meaningful community service.

Waldorf School Assistant

Assist teachers at Green Earth School with art, music, storytelling, sports, and reading with village children from kindergarten to grade 8.

Workshop Companion

Work alongside special adults in our Roshni Organic Bakery, Textile Workshop, Carpentry and Wood Workshop, or Art Workshop.

Farm & Gardening

Help with vegetable beds, composting, tree planting, irrigation, or dairy cow care on our chemical-free biodynamic farm.

Media, Photography & IT

Help us photograph village life, write newsletters, create social media content, or provide IT and administrative assistance.

Therapy & Special Care

Physiotherapists, speech therapists, psychologists, and medical practitioners offering specialized exercises and rehabilitation sessions.

Residential Co-Worker

Live on-site in Roshni Village for 3 to 12 months, sharing family meals, cultural festivals, and community rhythms with special adults.

What to Expect as a Volunteer

  • A Peaceful Oasis: Experience fresh air, organic food, and tranquility amidst the lush green surroundings off Bedian Road.
  • Transformative Connections: Deep friendships with special persons that change how you look at the world and human value.
  • Hands-on Learning: Learn Waldorf pedagogical approaches, organic sourdough baking, natural dyeing, and woodwork.
  • Certificate of Service: Official community service certificate issued upon completion of scheduled volunteer hours.
